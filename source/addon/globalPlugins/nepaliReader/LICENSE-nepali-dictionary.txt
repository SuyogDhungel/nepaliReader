nepaliFreq.dat (word counts, added in Nepali Reader 2.2.0)
Counts of the words in about 350,000 sentences of Nepali news collected by the nlp-doko project
(Team Naya, https://github.com/Team-Naya/nlp-doko, published as an open-source NLP database).
Only the words and how often each occurs are kept, not the sentences.
